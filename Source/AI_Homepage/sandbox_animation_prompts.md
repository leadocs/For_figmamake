# Sandbox 核心优势动画设计提示词 (Animation Prompts)

该文档为 Figma Make 或 Motion Designer 提供针对 "Agent Sandbox" 板块三个核心优势 Tab 的动画设计指南。

**整体视觉风格 (Visual Style):**
*   **Background:** Deep Obsidian Black (#0A0A0A)
*   **Accent Colors:** Electric Purple (#7C3AED) & Cyber Blue (#00A3FF)
*   **Style:** Abstract 3D, Isometric, Glowing Neon lines, Glassmorphism.

---

## 1. 虚拟机级安全隔离 (MicroVM Isolation)
**核心概念:** 独立、坚固、核心保护。
**对应文案:** "MicroVM Isolation - Kernel-level Security"

### 🎬 Animation Prompt
> **Subject:** A floating, translucent 3D glass cube containing a glowing, pulsating energy core (representing the Kernel).
> **Action:**
> 1.  The cube is initially open or wireframe.
> 2.  Thick, glowing security shields (panels) slide in from the sides to seal the cube hermetically.
> 3.  A "Lock" icon or a shield emblem manifests on the front face with a satisfying "click" animation.
> 4.  Faint red "threat" particles try to approach the cube but are bounced off by the glass shield.
> **Vibe:** Secure, Impenetrable, Clean.

---

## 2. 毫秒级极速启动 (Instant Scalability)
**核心概念:** 速度、爆发、从无到有。
**对应文案:** "Instant Scalability - Scale to Zero"

### 🎬 Animation Prompt
> **Subject:** A flat horizontal timeline or a dormant server rack node.
> **Action:**
> 1.  **State Zero:** The scene is dark/dim. A flat line represents "0 cost".
> 2.  **Trigger:** A "Request" signal (bright light) hits the center.
> 3.  **Explosion:** Instantly (in <0.1s), a complex 3D server infrastructure pops up (grows vertically) from the flat line, fully lit and active.
> 4.  **Loop:** The structure glows brightly for a second, then seamlessly shrinks back to the flat line (Sleep mode), demonstrating "Scale to Zero".
> **Vibe:** Snappy, Responsive, Electric.

---

## 3. 开箱即用环境 (Pre-configured Stack)
**核心概念:** 堆叠、集成、自动组装。
**对应文案:** "Pre-configured Stack - Dev Ready"

### 🎬 Animation Prompt
> **Subject:** Isometric floating layers representing the tech stack (Bottom to Top: GPU Driver -> CUDA -> Python -> App).
> **Action:**
> 1.  The layers start scattered vertically in space.
> 2.  They rapidly descend and snap together magnetically one by one.
> 3.  **Layer 1 (Base):** "NVIDIA Driver" (Green glow).
> 4.  **Layer 2:** "CUDA/PyTorch" (Orange/Purple glow).
> 5.  **Layer 3 (Top):** "Your Code" (White glow).
> 6.  When the stack completes, it pulses once and emits a "Checkmark" or "Ready" signal.
> **Vibe:** Satisfying, Organized, Modular.
