# Figma Make Style Prompt

**Theme**: Enterprise AI SaaS, Dark Mode, Cinematic, High Precision.

**Visual Style**:
*   **Background**: Deep dark background (Obsidian/Midnight Blue) with "Dark Gold" (#C5A065) or "Cyber Blue" overlay effects.
*   **Atmosphere**: Professional, Trustworthy, Technological, "Black Gold" aesthetic similar to premium enterprise consoles (like Aliyun Bailian).
*   **Elements**:
    *   **Glassmorphism**: High-quality glass cards with subtle borders and blur.
    *   **Data Visualization**: Fine lines, technical HUD elements, glowing data points.
    *   **Lighting**: Ambient lighting effects to highlight key areas (Hero, Cards).

**Layout Structure**:
1.  **Hero Section**:
    *   Center-aligned bold typography.
    *   Background features abstract 3D geometry or particle waves representing "Data Flow" and "Computing Power".
    *   Two prominent CTA buttons with glowing effects.
2.  **Modelverse Section (Grid)**:
    *   Card-based layout for models.
    *   Each card looks like a high-tech container or chip.
3.  **Sandbox Section (Split)**:
    *   Left: Text description.
    *   Right: A visual representation of a "Secure Box" or "MicroVM" isolation, possibly an isometric illustration.
4.  **Advantages Section (Cards)**:
    *   3-column grid.
    *   Clean, icon-driven cards with hover glow effects.

**Color Palette**:
*   **Primary**: Deep Blue / Midnight.
*   **Accent**: Gold (#D4AF37) for premium highlights, Cyan (#00FFFF) for tech details.
*   **Text**: White (Headings), Light Grey (Body).
